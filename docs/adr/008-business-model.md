# ADR 008: Business Model and Revenue Strategy

**Status**: Accepted  
**Date**: 2026-04-15  
**Deciders**: Executive Team

## Context

African Property Trust (TripleCheck) addresses Kenya's $2.5B annual land fraud crisis. Need sustainable revenue model that serves market while achieving profitability.

## Decision

**Three Revenue Streams**:

1. **Transaction Fees (40% of revenue)**: 2-3% commission on verified property transactions
   - Target: 100 transactions/month = KES 12.5M monthly

2. **Verification Services (35% of revenue)**: Tiered pricing model
   - Basic (KES 5,000): Automated AI verification
   - Premium (KES 15,000): AI + human expert + physical verification
   - Enterprise (KES 50,000+): Full audit + blockchain proof
   - Target: 750 verifications/month = KES 8M monthly

3. **B2B Enterprise Licensing (25% of revenue)**: Recurring subscriptions
   - Banks: KES 500K-2M/month (NPL recovery, collateral verification)
   - Real Estate Agencies: KES 200K-500K/month (white-label platform)
   - Government: KES 1-5M/month (land registry integration)
   - Target: 17 enterprise clients = KES 11M monthly

**Total Target**: KES 31.5M/month (~$240,000 USD)

## Consequences

### Positive
- Diversified revenue reduces risk
- ~20:1 LTV:CAC ratio (exceptional)
- 82% gross margins (SaaS-level)
- 1-3 month payback period
- Path to profitability in 18 months

### Negative
- Complex pricing structure to manage
- Need to serve multiple customer segments
- Requires different sales approaches per stream

## Unit Economics
- Customer Acquisition Cost: KES 3,500 ($27)
- Lifetime Value: KES 275,000 ($2,100)
- LTV:CAC Ratio: ~20:1
- Gross Margin: 82%
- Payback Period: 1-3 months

## Financial Projections
- Year 1: KES 225M ($1.7M) revenue, KES 45M ($345K) profit
- Year 2: KES 675M ($5.2M) revenue, KES 202M ($1.5M) profit
- Year 3: KES 2.025B ($15.5M) revenue, KES 810M ($6.2M) profit
