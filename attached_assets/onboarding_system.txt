## USAGE INSTRUCTIONS AND NOTES

### For HR Administrators:
- Update the Employee Master Tracker weekly
- Use individual employee sheets for detailed tracking
- Monitor the dashboard for compliance issues
- Set up automated reminders for critical tasks

### For Managers:
- Review your direct reports' progress weekly
- Complete manager-specific tasks on time
- Use the system to plan workload during transitions
- Provide feedback on process improvements

### For IT Security:
- Focus on access control and asset recovery
- Monitor system access post-departure
- Maintain security protocols during transitions
- Update technical requirements as needed

### For Employees:
- Reference your individual tracking sheet
- Complete required tasks by due dates
- Communicate any issues or delays promptly
- Provide feedback for process improvement

### SCALING CONSIDERATIONS:
- Add department-specific onboarding modules as needed
- Implement role-based access controls for sensitive data
- Consider integration with HRIS systems as you grow
- Regularly review and update checklists based on feedback

### COMPLIANCE NOTES:
- Ensure all legal requirements are met (I9, tax forms, etc.)
- Maintain audit trails for all processes
- Keep documentation for employment law compliance
- Regular review of data retention policies
