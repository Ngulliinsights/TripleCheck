## Part 2: Design A System For Onboarding & Offboarding (15%)

**System Design Philosophy:**

The onboarding and offboarding system should be checklist-driven, role-specific, and timeline-based to ensure consistency and completeness. Each process needs clear ownership, deadlines, and status tracking to prevent security gaps and improve employee experience.

**Key Components:**

**Onboarding Tracking:**
Pre-arrival preparations including IT equipment setup, workspace preparation, and documentation gathering. Day-one activities covering welcome orientation, system access provisioning, and initial introductions. Week-one integration including team meetings, policy reviews, and role-specific training. The system would track completion status, responsible parties, and escalation triggers for delayed items.

**Offboarding Tracking:**
Notice period management including transition planning, knowledge transfer sessions, and access audit initiation. Final week activities covering equipment return, final payments processing, and exit interviews. Post-departure tasks including access revocation, asset recovery, and documentation archival. Each step would have clear deadlines and accountability measures.

**Scalability Features:**
Role-based templates that can be customized for different positions, automated reminder systems for approaching deadlines, and integration capabilities with HR systems and IT infrastructure. The system would support bulk operations while maintaining individual tracking granularity.

**Risk Mitigation:**
Security-focused design ensuring all access is properly granted and revoked, compliance tracking for legal requirements, and comprehensive audit trails for accountability. Regular system reviews would identify process improvements and address emerging needs.
