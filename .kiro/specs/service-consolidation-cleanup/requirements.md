# Requirements Document - Kenya Land Platform Optimization v3.0
*Strategic Consolidation with Business Context Preservation*

## Introduction

This requirements document establishes a **strategic consolidation approach** that combines aggressive performance optimization with careful preservation of business-critical functionality. The platform will undergo systematic consolidation to eliminate genuine redundancy while preserving specialized implementations that serve distinct business contexts in Kenya's land verification ecosystem.

### Core Principle: **Intelligent Consolidation**

We distinguish between valuable specialization and wasteful redundancy, consolidating duplicate implementations while preserving business-specific optimizations. This approach delivers measurable performance improvements while maintaining all critical capabilities for Kenya's land verification processes.

## Strategic Objectives

### Performance Targets
- Bundle size reduction: ≥40% (from 2.3MB to <1.4MB)
- Build time improvement: ≥45% (from 4.2min to <2.5min)
- Test execution acceleration: ≥35% improvement
- Security score elevation: B+ to A+ rating

### Business Continuity Requirements
- Zero disruption to active land verification processes
- Preservation of Kenya-specific compliance features
- Maintenance of all regulatory reporting capabilities
- Continued support for all existing user workflows
